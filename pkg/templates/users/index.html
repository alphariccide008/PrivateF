About the Scholarship.

The Tosin Eniolorunda Foundation is a visionary initiative established by Tosin, CEO of Moniepoint.

Through its strategic initiatives, the Tosin Eniolorunda Foundation is committed to shaping a future where young people, both STEM enthusiasts and young entrepreneurs, have the tools and encouragement they need to thrive in their respective fields.

About the
Future Builders Fund.

The Future Builders Fund is a pilot scholarship that supports 14 top STEM students across seven Nigerian universities:

Obafemi Awolowo University
University of Abuja
University of Nigeria, Nsukka
University of Lagos
University of Maiduguri
Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria
University of Calabar

The Future Builders Fund is a first step in a long-term commitment to building the future by investing in those ready to shape it.

Eligibility Criteria.

Applicants must meet the following criteria to be considered for the scholarship.

You’re 25 years or younger
